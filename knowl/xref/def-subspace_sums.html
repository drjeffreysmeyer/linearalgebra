Definition 2.5.3 Subspace Sum.

If \(V\) is a vector space and \(W_1,W_2\subset V\) are subspaces, then their sum is the set
🔗
\begin{equation*} W_1+W_2:= \{ \vect{w}_1+\vect{w}_2\ | \ \vect{w}_1\in W_1, \vect{w}_2\in W_2\}. \end{equation*}
More generally, the sum of a finite collection of subspaces \(W_1,\ldots, W_r\subset V\) is
🔗
\begin{equation*} W_1+W_2+\cdots+W_r:= \{ \vect{w}_1+\vect{w}_2+\cdots + \vect{w}_r\ | \ \vect{w}_1\in W_1, \vect{w}_2\in W_2, \ldots, \vect{w}_r\in W_r \}. \end{equation*}
