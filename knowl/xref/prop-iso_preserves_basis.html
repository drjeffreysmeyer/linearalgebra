Proposition 3.6.3 Isomorphisms send bases to bases.

If \(\mathcal{B}=\{\vect{v}_1,\ldots, \vect{v}_n\}\subset V\) is a basis for \(V\text{,}\) and \(T:V\to W\) is an isomorphism, then the image of \(\mathcal{B}\text{,}\) \(T(\mathcal{B})=\{T(\vect{v}_1),\ldots, T(\vect{v}_n)\}\text{,}\) is a basis for \(W\text{.}\)
