Definition 2.3.1 The Set of Lists.

If \(F\) is a field and \(n\in \Z_+\text{,}\) then the set of lists of length \(n\text{,}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} F^n=\left\{\begin{bmatrix}v_1\\v_2\\ \vdots \\ v_n \end{bmatrix} \ \bigg| \ v_1,\ldots, v_n\in F\right\} \end{equation*}
comes with two binary operations:
🔗
\begin{equation*} +:\ F^n\times F^n \to F^n \hspace{3pc} \begin{bmatrix}v_1\\v_2\\ \vdots \\ v_n \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}w_1\\w_2\\ \vdots \\ w_n \end{bmatrix} \mapsto \begin{bmatrix}v_1+w_1\\v_2+w_2\\ \vdots \\ v_n+w_n \end{bmatrix} \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} \cdot:\ F\times F^n \to F^n \hspace{3pc} c\cdot \begin{bmatrix}v_1\\v_2\\ \vdots \\ v_n \end{bmatrix} \mapsto \begin{bmatrix}cv_1\\cv_2\\ \vdots \\ cv_n \end{bmatrix} \end{equation*}
We will say that addition and scaling are done entry-by-entry.
