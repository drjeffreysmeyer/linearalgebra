Definition 2.4.1 Subspace.

If \(V\) is a vector space over the field \(F\text{,}\) then a vector subspace of \(V\) is a subset \(W\subset V\) satisfying:
🔗
  1. (S0) \(W\) is nonempty,
    🔗
    🔗
  2. (S1) \(W\) closed under addition: if \(\vect{w}_1,\vect{w}_2\in W\text{,}\) then \(\vect{w}_1+\vect{w}_2 \in W\text{,}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. (S2) \(W\) is closed under scaling: if \(a \in F\) and \(\vect{w}\in W\text{,}\) then \(a\vect{w}\in W\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
Any subspace of \(V\) which is not \(V\) is called proper and denoted \(W\subsetneq V\text{.}\)
