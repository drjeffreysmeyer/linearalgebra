Definition 2.12.5 Dimension.

If \(V\) is finite dimensional, and \(S\) is an independent spanning set, then the dimension of \(V\) is \(|S|\text{.}\) In symbols, we write \(\dim V:=|S|\text{.}\) If \(V\) is not finite dimensional, we write \(\dim V =\infty\text{.}\) The dimension of the trivial subspace is taken to be 0.
