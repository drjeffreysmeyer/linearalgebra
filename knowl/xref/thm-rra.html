Theorem 2.9.1 Redundancy Reduction Algorithm (RRA).

If \(S=\{\vect{v}_1,\ldots, \vect{v}_n\}\subset V-\{\vect{0}\}\text{,}\) and \(S\) is dependent, then there exists a \(j>1\) such that
🔗
\begin{equation*} \spn(\{\vect{v}_1,\vect{v}_2,\ldots,\vect{v}_{j-1}\}) =\spn(\{\vect{v}_1,\vect{v}_2,\ldots,\vect{v}_{j-1},\vect{v}_{j}\}) \end{equation*}
and it follows then that \(\spn(S) = \spn(S-\vect{v}_j)\text{.}\) Furthermore, \(j\) may be taken to be the first index for which \(\{\vect{v}_1,\vect{v}_2,\ldots,\vect{v}_{j-1}\}\) is independent but \(\{\vect{v}_1,\vect{v}_2,\ldots,\vect{v}_{j-1},\vect{v}_{j}\}\) is dependent.
