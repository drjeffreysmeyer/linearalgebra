Definition 3.6.1 Isomorphism.

If \(V\) and \(W\) are vector spaces and there exists linear maps \(T:V\to W\) and \(T':W\to V\) such that \(T\circ T'=\id_{W}\) and \(T'\circ T=\id_{V}\text{,}\) then \(T\) is a linear isomorphism
Isomorphism is a word used throughout mathematics in different contexts. The word linear clarifies the type of isomorphism. However, in this course, since all isomorphisms will be linear, I will often just say “isomorphism.”
