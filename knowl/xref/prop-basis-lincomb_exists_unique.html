Proposition 2.13.4 Every vector can be written uniquely as a linear combination of basis vectors.

If \(\mathcal{B}= \{\vect{v}_1,\ldots , \vect{v}_n\} \subset V\) is an ordered basis and \(\vect{w}\in V\text{,}\) then there exists a unique collection of scalars \(x_1,\ldots, x_n\in F\) such that \(\vect{w}=x_1\vect{v}_1+\cdots +x_n\vect{v}_n\text{.}\)
