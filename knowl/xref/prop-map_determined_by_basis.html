Proposition 3.4.3 A map is uniquely determined by what it does to a basis.

If \(V\) is a finite dimensional vector space with ordered basis, \(\mathcal{B}=\{\vect{v}_1,\ldots, \vect{v}_n\}\subset V\) , \(W\) is a vector space, and \(T_1,T_2:V\to W\) are linear maps such that for each \(\vect{v}_i\in \mathcal{B}\text{,}\) \(T_1(\vect{v}_i)= T_2(\vect{v}_i)\text{,}\) then \(T_1=T_2\text{.}\)
