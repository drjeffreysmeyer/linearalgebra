Proposition 3.7.3 The coordinate map and mixture map are inverses.

If \(\mathcal{B}=\{\vect{v}_1,\ldots, \vect{v}_n\}\subset V\) is an ordered basis, then
🔗
\begin{equation*} \Coord_\mathcal{B} \circ \Mix_\mathcal{B}=\id_{F^n} \quad \text{and}\quad \Mix_\mathcal{B} \circ \Coord_\mathcal{B}=\id_{V}. \end{equation*}
